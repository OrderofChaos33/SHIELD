# Layer 5 — Mirrorfield (active defense, own perimeter only)

Added 2026-11-05. Invariants: INV-L5-01…07 in `docs/INVARIANTS.md`.
Code: `crates/shield-core/src/mirrorfield.rs`. Full-loop proof:
`crates/shield-core/tests/mirrorfield_loop.rs`.

## The legal line (founder rule, 2026-11-05: never break the law, ever)

Mirrorfield defends the owner's own stack. It contains **nothing**
that interferes with, disables, deauthenticates, jams, or degrades
another party's device or communications. Its whole arsenal operates
on assets the owner controls: our advertisements, our GATT surface,
our grants, our audit log, our release decisions.

## The second rule: NO FREE DATA (founder, 2026-11-05)

No consumer without an existing `features` grant receives anything —
real, mirror, or decoy bytes. The mirror is a *degraded service under
a grant*, not a handout to whoever probes us. Ungranted peers get
`NoGrant` and zero bytes, in every posture.

## Components

**Probe detector.** Scores per-peer behavior against our stack:
connection attempts, pairing attempts, service discovery, reads,
writes, reconnect bursts, malformed frames. Reading ≥6 distinct
characteristics inside 10 s counts as enumeration. Scores and
verdicts are monotonic (INV-L5-06) — a peer never talks its way back
down.

**Canary characteristics + decoy persona.** The persona owns decoy
GATT characteristics that are never served to legitimate consumers.
Touching one is definitive: instant Hostile verdict + quarantine,
audit-logged before any response (INV-L5-01). Each canary carries a
watermark (`sha256(persona_seed ‖ uuid)`, truncated) that is unique
per characteristic and stable per seed — if decoy data ever leaks,
the watermark attributes which decoy path served it (INV-L5-05).
Persona IDs rotate hourly.

**Advertisement hardening.** `sanitize_advertisement` strips our own
broadcast identity: no device name, no manufacturer data, no tx
power, only the primary service UUID (INV-L5-04).

**Posture state machine.** Normal → Alert (first Suspicious verdict
or quarantine) → Lockdown (owner panic only). In Lockdown, granted
consumers receive mirror features only; ungranted receive nothing.
Lockdown exits only via explicit owner clear, also audit-logged —
probe traffic can never clear it (INV-L5-07). Owner clear does not
forgive anyone: scores and quarantines persist.

**Mirror service.** A quarantined-but-granted consumer keeps
"working" — against synthetic features generated by the persona.
Mirror values are a pure function of (persona seed, serve counter);
the mirror path never reads the real frame (INV-L5-03), so there is
nothing real to extract, time, or correlate. Mirror releases are
audit-logged under scope `features:mirror` at ε = 0.15 (the maximum),
so any verifier reading a receipt can see exactly what kind of
release it was. No silent deception of verifiers — only of probers,
and only with data that was never real.

## What is NOT here (honest limits)

- Passive sniffing of public BLE advertisements by a third party
  cannot be detected from the phone; Mirrorfield detects *active*
  probing of our stack. Advertisement hardening shrinks what passive
  capture yields.
- The detector consumes probe events; wiring it to a live btleplug
  central/peripheral event stream is Phase 2 hardware work, same as
  the rest of the radio path.
- Posture currently lives in core state; the Android shell's panic
  UX (one-tap lockdown, radio-quiet prompts) is Phase 2.
